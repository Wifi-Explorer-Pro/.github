## What is WiFi Explorer Pro?

WiFi Explorer Pro, from Intuitibits, is the professional edition of WiFi Explorer, aimed at network administrators, consultants, and IT professionals. It goes beyond a basic network list to provide in-depth analysis of the wireless environment, helping identify interference, poor coverage, channel conflicts, and configuration issues. It is a go-to tool for anyone who manages or troubleshoots Wi-Fi networks.

WiFi Explorer Pro is a native Mac app that runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

## Scan and analyze networks

WiFi Explorer Pro discovers the wireless networks in range and presents rich details for each: SSID, signal strength (RSSI), noise, channel, band, security, and supported data rates. Graphs show signal over time and channel usage, so you can quickly spot which networks are crowding a channel or where a signal is weak.

## Troubleshoot Wi-Fi problems

The app is designed for diagnosis. By visualizing signal quality, channel overlap, and interference, it helps you find the cause of dropouts, slow speeds, and dead zones. You can use it to choose the best channel, position access points, and verify that a network is configured and performing as expected.
